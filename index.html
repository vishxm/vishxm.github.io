04 / Working style

I care about clarity.

Good data work should make things easier to understand. The right model makes a metric obvious, the right test catches a problem early, and the right documentation helps a team move without waiting for one person.

My approach is practical: understand who is making the decision, trace the data back to its source, then build a dependable path between the two.

“The best data systems make the right thing easier to see.”
